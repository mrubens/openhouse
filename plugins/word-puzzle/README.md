# Word puzzle

A word a day for kids about 6 to 10: its letters mixed up, and a clue.

- **Today:** a small card with the letters (turn it on in Tabs and look, Cards on Home).
- **The voice:** "what's today's word puzzle?", "give me a hint" (what kind of thing it is, then the clue, the first
  letter, the last), "is it penguin?", "what's the answer?".
- **The page:** big letter tiles, hints one at a time, and Show the word.
- Everyone in the household gets the same word that day. Eighty words, in a different order each year.

## What it asks for

- a card of its own on Home
- it can't reach the internet
