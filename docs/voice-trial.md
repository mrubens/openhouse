# The two live voices, side by side

Thirty asks, said the same way to OpenAI's live voice and to Google's (Gemini Live, on the household's own Gemini
key: docs/assistant.md), so the two can be compared on the same household and the same device. Write down, for each
voice, whether it did the right thing and how many seconds it took to start answering. The names below are the
sample household's (Sam, Alex, Maya, Leo in Springfield); say your own household's names instead.

## Before you start

- **Both keys in:** Settings → Opie → Voice → Your own keys. An OpenAI key, or Opie's cloud voice, for OpenAI's; a
  Gemini key from a project with billing on, for Google's.
- **Switching voices:** Settings → Opie → Voice. A Google voice (Kore, Puck…) is Gemini; any other voice is OpenAI's.
  The next conversation uses the voice you picked.
- **The same room for both:** the same distance from the device, the same volume, no music or TV playing (except for
  the TV asks).
- **Order:** do asks 1 to 15 with OpenAI first and 16 to 30 with Gemini first, so neither voice always gets the
  household's things in the state the other left them.
- **Each ask is its own conversation:** "Hey Opie", then the ask in one breath. Wait for it to finish and go quiet
  before the next one.
- **Seconds:** from when you stop talking to its first word. A phone's stopwatch is fine; round to the half second.
- **Right:** it did what the last column says, and said so plainly. Anything else is wrong: the wrong thing done,
  nothing done, made up, or asked again when the ask was clear. Note why in a few words.
- **Afterwards:** tidy what the asks added (the test items, events and timers).
