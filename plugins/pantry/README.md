# Pantry

What's on hand, in two columns, kept up by voice or by tap.

- **The voice:** "we have two bags of frozen peas in the freezer", "we used the last of the rice", "what's in the
  freezer?", "do we have black beans?", "what can we make for dinner?" and then "let's do the chili".
- **Dinner ideas:** only the household's own meals (its recipe box and favorites in Meals), each weighed against
  what's here by Opie's small judgments (the household's cloud, from its monthly allowance). Tonight puts one on
  tonight's dinner in Meals. Without the cloud, it falls back to recipes whose ingredients name something on hand.
- **Set up:** none. Ideas need some meals in Meals.

## What it asks for

- meals (to read the recipe box and favorites, and to set tonight's dinner)
- small questions to Opie's cloud (to weigh meals against what's here, and to tell which item "the peas" means)
- it can't reach the internet
