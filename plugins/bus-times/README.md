# Bus times

When the next bus really comes to your stop, live from your transit agency.

- **Set up:** open Bus times, Set up.
  1. Find your agency in the catalog (about 600 agencies with live feeds, from the Mobility Database), or paste its live
     feed's address (GTFS-realtime trip updates) and its timetable's (a GTFS zip) from the agency's developer page.
     Some agencies give each person a key: put yours in, and its name in the address if the catalog doesn't know it.
  2. Use this agency: the tablet asks, with the PIN, "Let Bus times reach <the agency's website>?". Only on a yes can
     the app read from it. Then it reads the agency's stops.
  3. Find your stop by its name or the number on its sign, and choose the days and hours to show it on Home (school
     mornings, 7:20 to 8:05, to start) and what the line is called ("School bus").
- **Today:** in those hours only, one line: "School bus: 4 at 7:42, then 104 at 7:58" (not on the sleep screen). A
  small card for Home shows the minutes.
- **The voice:** "when's the next bus?"
- Your stop and key stay on the tablet. The agency is asked once a minute in the hours you chose, or while the app is
  open.

## What it asks for

- a line of its own on Home
- a card of its own on Home
- reaching `files.mobilitydatabase.org` (the catalog's copies of agencies' timetables)
- a website you choose when you set it up: your transit agency's
