# Trivia night

A family quiz with Opie as the host.

- **Playing:** say "let's play trivia" (or "trivia with Sam and Dad, 15 questions"). The board opens with the question
  and four choices, A to D; Opie reads it out. Everyone calls out a letter ("Sam says B"); once everyone has answered,
  Opie gives the answer and who got it. "Next", "who's winning?", "read it again", "show the answer", "end the game".
  Taps work too: a name, then their letter.
- **The questions:** sixty for families with kids about 6 and up, a different deal each game.
- **Set up:** none. It reads the family's names for the players.

**On the TV:** not yet. A TV layout from a plugin (`"screens": ["tv"]`) would replace the TV's own home layout for as
long as it's installed, and a TV app can't read its own app's data yet, so it couldn't follow the game. Trivia night
plays on the tablet until Opie can show a TV app only while it's in use.

## What it asks for

- the family's names (the players)
- it can't reach the internet
