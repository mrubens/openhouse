# Opie

Your household's day, calendar, lists, chores, meals, photos and apps on every phone, computer and TV in the house,
with Opie to ask. It runs here, on your Home Assistant, and uses it already: pick the lights, switches, scenes and
media players Opie may use. Locks, doors, alarms and cameras stay out of its reach.

It is open source: https://github.com/mrubens/openhouse
