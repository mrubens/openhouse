# Reading log

How much each reader reads, day by day.

- **The voice:** "Maya read 20 minutes", "Sam read half an hour of Wings of Fire last night", "how much has Maya
  read this week?". The voice hands over who, how many minutes and the book; the app keeps the log.
- **The page:** a column for each reader with today's minutes, their streak (days in a row at the daily goal), this
  week as bars with the goal as a line, and the latest entries (an x takes one off). Add reading by hand there too.
- **Today:** a wide card with each reader's week as small bars (turn it on in Tabs and look, Cards on Home).
- **Stars (optional):** in Goal and stars. Each reader gets a "Reading" chore in Chores that ticks itself the day they
  reach the goal, so the star is a normal Chores star and counts toward their rewards. Turning stars off takes the
  chore away. If you take the plugin off with stars on, the Reading chores stay in Chores: take them off there.
- **Set up:** none needed. It reads the family's names; Goal and stars picks the readers (everyone, to start) and the
  daily goal (20 minutes).

## What it asks for

- the family's names (to know the readers)
- chores (only for the optional stars: it adds, ticks and removes its own Reading chore)
- a card of its own on Home
- it can't reach the internet
