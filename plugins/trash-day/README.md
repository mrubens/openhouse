# Trash day

Which night the bins go out.

- **Set up:** open Trash day, Set up: the pickup day and whether the truck comes early in the morning or at a time; whether recycling goes every week or every other week (and a
  day in one of its weeks); yard waste or compost, if it's picked up, with its weeks and months; and whether a weekday
  holiday moves the rest of the week a day later (the six usual US holidays: New Year's Day, Memorial Day, the Fourth
  of July, Labor Day, Thanksgiving and Christmas, when they fall on a weekday).
- **Or the city's calendar:** if the city has a pickup calendar to subscribe to (ReCollect, or a Google, iCloud or
  Outlook calendar), paste its link: its own dates and words are used, holidays and all.
- **The calendar:** each pickup is a note on its day ("Trash · Trash and recycling go out").
- **Today:** one line, only when there's something to do. For an early pickup (the default), from 5 PM the evening
  before: "Trash and recycling out tonight" (on the sleep screen too, until the truck comes). For a later one, the
  morning of: "Trash out by 1:00 PM". Then, once it's been (from noon), "Bring the bins back in" until 9 PM, unless
  that's turned off. An x on the line takes it down. A holiday that moves the pickup moves these too. There's a small
  card for Home as well, with the next pickup.
- **The voice:** "when's trash day?", "is it recycling week?", "when does yard waste go out?".

Nothing is sent anywhere with the schedule; with a city calendar, its link is read twice a day.

## What it asks for

- a line of its own on Home
- notes of its own on the calendar's days
- a card of its own on Home
- reaching the calendar hosts a city pickup calendar can be on: `api.recollect.net`, `recollect.a.ssl.fastly.net`,
  `calendar.google.com`, `*.icloud.com`, `outlook.office365.com`, `outlook.live.com`
