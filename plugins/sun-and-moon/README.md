# Daylight

Today's sunrise and sunset, how long the day is and whether it's getting longer, tomorrow's times, and the moon's
phase with the next full and new moon.

- **Today:** a small card with the sunset (the sunrise before dawn) and tonight's moon. Turn it on in Tabs and look,
  Cards on Today.
- **The voice:** "when's sunset?", "what time is sunrise tomorrow?", "how much daylight do we get today?", "is it a
  full moon tonight?"
- **Set up:** none. It uses the tablet's own weather for today's sunrise and sunset, so the weather needs to know
  where home is.

Nothing is fetched. Tomorrow's times come from working back from today's sunrise and sunset to roughly where home is,
then forward to another day; the moon's phase needs no place. Good to a minute or two.

## What it asks for

- the weather (for today's sunrise and sunset)
- a card of its own on Today
- it can't reach the internet
